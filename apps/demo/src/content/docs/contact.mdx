Sample Co is a fictional company, so the `weather.example` addresses below don't receive mail. They show what a product's contact page would carry. For this site itself, open an issue or a discussion on the [Nimbus repository](https://github.com/cloudflare/nimbus), which builds and runs it.

| For | Contact | Answer within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| API support, errors and outages | `support@weather.example` | 1 business day; quote the `request_id` from the error |
| Plans, limits and invoices | `sales@weather.example` | 2 business days |
| Security reports | `security@weather.example` | 1 business day; see the [security policy](https://github.com/cloudflare/nimbus/security) for this site |
| Press | `press@weather.example` | 3 business days |

## Before you write

Most questions are answered faster by the docs:

- An error response explains itself: its `type`, `code` and `message` are listed in [Errors](/concepts/errors).
- Limits and usage are in your account: [Rate limits](/concepts/rate-limits) shows how to read them.

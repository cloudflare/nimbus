Sample Co makes the Weather API: forecasts, observations, alerts, imagery and climate history for any point on Earth, over one REST API. Teams use it to show the weather in their apps, to time work outdoors (spraying a field, pouring concrete, launching a drone), and to warn people before severe weather arrives.

The data comes from 40,000 ground stations, three satellite families and the radar network, blended by three forecast models into one answer per point. [How forecasts are made](/concepts/forecasts) follows that pipeline from a station reading to the numbers in a response.

Sample Co is fictional, and so is its API at `api.weather.example`. This site is the reference demo for [Nimbus](https://nimbus-docs.com), the documentation framework it's built with: a product's docs with every agent feature on, from Markdown at every URL to an MCP server. Its source is open, in [`apps/demo`](https://github.com/cloudflare/nimbus/tree/main/apps/demo) of the Nimbus repository.

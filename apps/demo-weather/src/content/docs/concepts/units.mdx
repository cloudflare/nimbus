Every measurement in a response is in one unit system, and you choose which.

## Choose a unit system

Pass `units` on any endpoint that returns measurements. The response echoes the choice in its `units` field, so stored responses stay self-describing.

```sh
curl "https://api.weather.example/v2/forecasts/daily?latitude=40.7128&longitude=-74.006&units=imperial" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WEATHER_API_KEY"
```

## Units by measurement

| Measurement | `metric` (default) | `imperial` | `si` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | °C | °F | K |
| Wind speed | m/s | mph | m/s |
| Precipitation | mm | in | mm |
| Snowfall | cm | in | m |
| Visibility | km | mi | m |
| Pressure | hPa | hPa | Pa |

## Values that never change units

- **Times** are always UTC in ISO 8601.
- **Coordinates** are always WGS 84 decimal degrees.
- **Directions** are always degrees true, from 0 to 359.
- **Readings you submit** from a private station are always SI, whatever `units` you read them back in. See [Report from your own station](/guides/private-stations).

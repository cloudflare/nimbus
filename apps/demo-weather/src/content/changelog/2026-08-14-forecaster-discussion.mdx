The reasoning behind the forecast, in the forecaster's words.

<ReleaseSection title="New">


- [Get the forecaster discussion](/api/forecasts/get-forecast-discussion/) returns the written reasoning behind the regional forecast: the patterns the forecaster is watching, where models disagree, and how confident they are. It updates with every forecast cycle, up to four times a day.

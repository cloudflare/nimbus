Get alerts pushed to your server instead of polling for them.

<ReleaseSection title="New">


- [Create a subscription](/api/alerts/create-subscription/) for your zones and the events you want, and each new, updated, or cancelled alert arrives at your endpoint as a signed webhook. See [Build a rain alert](/guides/rain-alerts).

```json
{
  "url": "https://example.com/hooks/weather",
  "events": ["alert.issued", "alert.updated", "alert.cancelled"],
  "zones": ["zone_no_03"],
  "min_severity": "moderate"
}
```

- Verify each delivery with its `Webhook-Signature` header. See [Webhook events](/resources/webhook-events#verify-the-signature).

</ReleaseSection>

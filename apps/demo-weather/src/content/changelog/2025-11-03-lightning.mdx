Lightning data for outdoor-safety decisions, and a faster export format.

<ReleaseSection title="New">


- [Get lightning proximity](/api/lightning/get-lightning-proximity/) returns the nearest strike in the last 30 minutes, its distance and bearing, and whether activity is approaching. Built for "clear the field" decisions at stadiums, pools, and worksites.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection title="Improved">


- Exports can now be written as Parquet as well as CSV and JSON. Set `format: "parquet"` when you create the export.

</ReleaseSection>

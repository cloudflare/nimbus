A new agriculture endpoint, and a replacement for the ping endpoint.

<ReleaseSection title="New">


- [Get spray windows](/api/agriculture/get-spray-windows/) returns the hours when wind, rain, and temperature allow crop spraying at a field, ranked by drift risk.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection title="Deprecated">


- `GET /ping`. Use [`GET /status`](/api/service/get-service-status/) instead: it reports the same liveness and adds per-region status. `GET /ping` keeps working until v3.

</ReleaseSection>
